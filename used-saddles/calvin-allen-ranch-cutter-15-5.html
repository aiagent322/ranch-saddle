Calvin Allen Saddlery out of Weatherford, Texas — the center of cutting and ranch horse country — built honest, functional saddles that serve working cowboys and competitive riders equally. This Ranch Cutter is exactly that: full basketweave tooling across every panel, braided rawhide horn wrap that's ready to use, antiqued berry conchos, and woolskin lining with even contact wear that tells you it was fitted and used correctly.

The stamp reads clearly: Custom Made / Calvin Allen / Weatherford, TX. At 15½ inches this is a compact seat with the geometry to cross over between ranch work and cutting — the Calvin Allen ranch cutter design was built to do both. Solid construction, honest condition, priced for the rider who wants a known-maker working saddle at a working-person price. Contact David to discuss tree width for your horse.
