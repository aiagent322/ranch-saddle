About Andy Mashke & Superior Saddlery

Andy Mashke has spent decades building performance saddles trusted by NRHA and NCHA competitors at the highest levels of the sport. His four ranch saddle builds apply the same SYMMETREES™ encapsulated wood tree technology — glass over hardwood for moisture resistance and dimensional stability — to a working ranch platform. Every saddle leaves his shop with H/O skirting leather, hand-fitted gullet, and the quality control that separates a competition build from a production saddle. Contact Superior Saddlery directly for current pricing, availability, and custom build options.
